# Acme Cloud: Support Knowledge Base (fictional company, synthetic data)

## Plans and prices (billed monthly; annual billing gives 2 months free)
- Free: $0. 1 project, 1 user, 1 GB storage, community support only.
- Starter: $12 per user per month. 10 projects, 50 GB storage, email support.
- Business: $39 per user per month. Unlimited projects, 1 TB storage, SSO, priority email and chat support.
- Enterprise: custom pricing. Dedicated account manager, audit logs, 99.95% uptime SLA. Contact sales.

## Refunds and billing
- Refund window: 30 days from the first payment on a paid plan. After that, no refunds.
- Annual plans: refundable in full within 30 days, then not refundable. Cancelling stops renewal only.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method in 5-7 business days.
- Invoices are emailed on the billing date and are available under Settings > Billing.
- Accepted payment: credit/debit card. Purchase orders only on Enterprise.
- Customers must never send full card numbers to support. Card details are only entered on the secure checkout page.

## Supported integrations
- Slack, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Google Drive, Zapier.
- Salesforce integration is on the roadmap with no release date.
- Public REST API is available on Starter and above.

## Data retention and security
- Active account data is kept for as long as the account is open.
- After account deletion, data is permanently removed within 30 days.
- Deleted files stay in the trash for 14 days.
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
- Acme Cloud does not sell customer data.

## Support hours
- Email support: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 UTC. First reply within 1 business day.
- Live chat (Business and Enterprise): Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 UTC.
- No phone support. No weekend support, except Enterprise customers for severity-1 outages.

## Escalation rules
- Outages, security incidents and billing disputes over $500 are escalated to a human agent.
- Customers who ask for a human, a manager or a supervisor are handed to a human agent via support@acmecloud.example.
- Legal questions (contracts, GDPR requests, compliance certificates) go to legal@acmecloud.example.
